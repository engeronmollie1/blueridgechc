Patient-Centered Medical Home

Our Comprehensive Care Services

We offer a complete continuum of primary medical care, mental health counseling, laboratory diagnostics, and patient assistance programs all under one roof in Staunton.

Comprehensive Primary Care

Annual wellness exams, well-child visits, physicals for school and work, and timely care for acute conditions such as the flu, sinus infections, bronchitis, and minor cuts.

  • Routine Adult & Pediatric Checkups
  • Adult & Childhood Immunizations
  • On-Site Minor Wound Management

Chronic Disease Management

Specialized disease stabilization protocols to help patients monitor and treat long-term illnesses, reducing costly hospitalizations and emergency visits.

  • Diabetes (Type 1 & 2) A1c Monitoring
  • Hypertension & Cholesterol Control
  • Asthma & COPD Respiratory Plans

Integrated Behavioral Health

Compassionate, non-judgmental counseling and psychiatric medication consultations delivered collaboratively with your primary physician in the same clinic visit.

  • Individual Cognitive Behavioral Therapy
  • Depression & Anxiety Medication Mgmt
  • Substance Use Harm Reduction & Support

Prescription & 340B Pharmacy

Access deeply discounted medications through our federal 340B drug pricing program partnership, ensuring no patient abandons a critical prescription due to expense.

  • Sliding Scale Prescription Co-pays
  • Free Medication Courier for Homebound
  • Patient Assistance Program (PAP) Enrollment

Rural Outreach & Mobile Unit

Our equipped mobile medical bus visits rural Augusta County communities, churches, and community centers, providing direct health screenings and enrollment clinics.

  • Free Community Health Fairs & Screenings
  • Medicaid / SNAP Benefits Application Aid
  • Spanish Language Interpretation Services

Virtual Telehealth Visits

Secure, HIPAA-compliant video and telephone visits for patients with transportation barriers, allowing consultation with clinicians from the safety of home.

  • Behavioral Health Tele-Counseling
  • Chronic Refill Evaluations
  • Zero App Download Required
Understanding the Fee Scale

Need help with healthcare costs?

Bring proof of income (such as a recent pay stub, W-2, or social security letter) to your first appointment. Based on household size, primary care visits range from a nominal $15 fee to standard insurance copays.
