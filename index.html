Equitable Community Care

Comprehensive Mountain Clinic Services

We deliver an integrated spectrum of clinical and social services designed to heal the whole person, reduce unnecessary emergency visits, and uplift families across the Blue Ridge.

Adult Primary & Mountain Rural Health Care

Comprehensive check-ups, continuous diabetes management (A1C testing, glucometers), hypertension regulation, asthma management, and preventative cardiology for working-class adults.

  • Routine Physicals & Lab Work
  • Free Glucose & Cholesterol Screenings
  • Personalized Health Education Plans

Blue Ridge Pediatrics & Well-Child Checks

Gentle, nurturing care for newborns, toddlers, and school-age children. We ensure Appalachian children stay healthy, hit developmental milestones, and attend school fully protected.

  • Vaccines for Children (VFC Program at $0)
  • School & Sports Physical Documentation
  • Pediatric Vision & Hearing Screenings

Women's Health & Prenatal Support

Compassionate reproductive health, prenatal checkups, post-partum maternal wellness, cervical Pap smears, breast health evaluations, and confidential family planning guidance.

  • Prenatal Ultrasound & Lab Coordination
  • Contraception Counseling & Education
  • Low-Cost Cancer Screenings

340B Discount Prescription Program

Under the federal 340B Drug Pricing Program, our patients receive essential medications—including insulin, inhalers, blood pressure pills, and antibiotics—at up to 90% below retail costs.

  • $4 to $10 Common Prescriptions
  • Direct Mountain Partner Pharmacy Delivery
  • Medication Therapy Management by Pharmacists

Mental Health Counseling & Addiction Support

Trauma-informed behavioral therapy led by our 3 Licensed Social Workers. We help individuals and families overcome stress, depression, anxiety, grief, and substance dependence with empathy.

  • 1-on-1 Confidential Psychotherapy
  • Medication-Assisted Treatment (MAT) Support
  • Free Narcan Kits & Community Overdose Training

Food, Heating & NC Medicaid Enrollment

Medical care cannot succeed without adequate food or winter heat. Our navigators enroll eligible mountain families in NC Medicaid, SNAP food stamps, and emergency winter energy assistance.

  • On-Site NC Medicaid Application Assistance
  • Emergency Winter Heating Grants & Woodbank Referrals
  • Rural Transit Vouchers & Food Box Coordination
Fair & Transparent Pricing

How Our Sliding-Fee Scale Works

Discounts are determined strictly by Federal Poverty Guidelines (FPG), based on family size and household income. No proof of legal status is ever requested.

Income Level (% of Poverty Line) Doctor Consultation Basic Lab Work Prescription Copay
Under 100% FPG (Nominal Fee) $10 Flat Fee $0 (Included) $4 or Free
101% – 150% FPG $20 Flat Fee $10 Lab Copay $5 – $10
151% – 200% FPG $35 Flat Fee $15 Lab Copay Tiered 340B Cost
Over 200% FPG or Insured Standard Medicaid / Ins. Billed to Plan Plan Formulary

*If you have zero income or are in a severe mountain hardship, fees are completely waived ($0). Nobody is turned away.
